Privacy notice

Effective 26 November 2026 · Version 2026-11-26

This notice explains how Global Software Solution uses information supplied through this website. For privacy questions or requests, contact knightndgale@gmail.com and copy jay.sterling@mail.com.

What you provide

The inquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional company or project name, selected service and project message. It also sends your agreement acknowledgment, the terms version and the website page address. We do not request payment details, account passwords, identity documents or file uploads.

Why we use it and who receives it

We use your inquiry to understand your project request and respond. FormSubmit processes the form and routes it to Mark at knightndgale@gmail.com, with Jay at jay.sterling@mail.com copied. Your email address is used as the reply address. An inquiry is not a marketing subscription.

Hosting, delivery and provider data

GitHub Pages serves the website and receives ordinary web-request information. FormSubmit receives the submitted fields and may process technical request data to operate its service and filter abuse. The recipients’ email providers process and store the delivered email. These providers may process information in countries other than your own.

FormSubmit’s documentation states that it retains form submissions in its archive for 30 days. That is the provider’s archive period, not a deletion promise for email already delivered to us. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy, its documentation and the GitHub privacy statement.

Retention and your requests

We keep inquiry correspondence while it is reasonably needed to respond, discuss the project and retain any records required by applicable law. If no project proceeds, you may ask us to delete the inquiry. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion, or to object to further contact. We may need to confirm your identity and explain any legal reason a record must be retained. Your rights depend on the laws that apply to you.

Cookies, storage and optional email links

Our website code does not use analytics, advertising scripts, cookies, local storage or session storage. Photos and fonts are hosted with the website. Unsaved inquiry text stays in the open page until you submit, navigate away or close it. If you choose an email link, your email application handles the message instead of the form.

Protecting your information

We use HTTPS for the website and form delivery. No public website or email service can guarantee complete security. Please send a general outline first and use an agreed channel for confidential project material. To report a suspected issue, email us with a description and avoid posting private data publicly.

Changes

We will update this notice when the website’s data handling changes. The effective date above identifies the current version.
