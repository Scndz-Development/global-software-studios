Inquiry privacy

This website does not use analytics or tracking scripts. When you submit an inquiry, your name, email, optional company, selected service and message are sent to FormSubmit for processing and delivery to knightndgale@gmail.com, with jay.sterling@mail.com copied. Your email is used as the reply address.

FormSubmit states that submissions are retained for 30 days. See the FormSubmit privacy policy and service documentation for its processing and verification details. We use your inquiry to respond to your project request. Please avoid including passwords or confidential files in the initial message.

Unsaved form entries remain only in the current page; this website does not store them in browser storage. If you choose the email link instead, your email app handles sending the message.
