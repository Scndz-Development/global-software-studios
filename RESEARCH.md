# Website content and structure research

Reviewed primary studio websites on 26 November 2026. These are structural references; the site uses original wording, its own project evidence and its own visual system.

| Source | Useful pattern | Applied to this site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [thoughtbot — Services](https://thoughtbot.com/services) | Explain support for different product stages and connect design with implementation. | Added three starting points: shaping an idea, designing a product and improving an existing application. Each preselects a matching inquiry category. |
| [thoughtbot — Product design](https://thoughtbot.com/services/product-design) | Discuss complete user flows and concrete design activities. | Replaced generic service copy with journeys, wireframes, prototypes and reusable interface patterns. |
| [Netguru — Services](https://www.netguru.com/services) | Make discovery, design and development distinct and understandable. | Added typical deliverables to each service, so visitors can see what the work produces. |
| [ustwo — Work](https://ustwo.com/work/) | Introduce case studies through the specific product purpose and challenge. | Made PNTZE, SCNDZ and CFS summaries more concrete while preserving their actual ongoing status. |

No client lists, awards, years of experience, results, testimonials or delivery timelines were adopted from these companies. Source project logos were taken from the user's project materials, not from the benchmark sites.

## Inquiry transport reference

[FormSubmit documentation](https://formsubmit.co/documentation) defines primary email endpoints, `_cc`, Reply-To, honeypot fields, custom return pages and AJAX delivery. Its [setup guide](https://formsubmit.co/) describes the required one-time recipient activation. The site distinguishes email-service acceptance from confirmed inbox delivery and keeps a direct-email alternative available.
